# Sudbury Slice: Storyboard
**Persona:** Dana Lavoie, a night-shift nurse at Health Sciences North who is vegetarian and always starving at 7 p.m.

### Panel 1: Browsing the menu
**Scene:** Dana opens the app on her break, tired and hungry, wanting something quick that has no meat.
**Screen:** Main screen, pizza list.
**Data used:** Reads pizzas (name, category, base price).

### Panel 2: Checking what is on it
**Scene:** She taps Garden Party and wants to be sure there is nothing she does not eat.
**Screen:** Main screen, pizza details panel.
**Data used:** Reads pizzas, toppings and the pizza-topping links (topping, portion size, vegan flag, extra cost).

### Panel 3: Reading what others think
**Scene:** Dana scrolls down to see whether other customers liked it before she commits her dinner break to it.
**Screen:** Main screen, ratings list.
**Data used:** Reads ratings (stars, remark, date) and customers (first name).

### Panel 4: Rating after dinner
**Scene:** Back home, full and happy, Dana gives Garden Party 5 stars and a short remark.
**Screen:** Main screen, rate-this-pizza panel.
**Data used:** Writes a new rating for one customer and one pizza (stars, date, remark).

### Panel 5: Moose sees the result
**Scene:** Moose checks the leaderboard on his phone and smiles when Garden Party climbs the ranks.
**Screen:** Main screen, ratings summary.
**Data used:** Reads ratings and pizzas (average stars per pizza).
